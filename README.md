PAGE 3: ALROHAIMI THEORY

Section 1 — What is Alrohaimi Theory? Alrohaimi Theory is a foundational intellectual system exploring the movement from latency to cognition, meaning, decision, transformation, and impact. It represents one of the Center's core intellectual projects — a systematic attempt to understand how human potential moves from a latent state through the stages of cognition and meaning-making to action, transformation, and measurable impact. The theory serves as the anchor for all the Center's research, programs, and applied work.

The theory progression: Latent Potential → Cognition → Meaning → Decision → Transformation → Impact

Section 2 — The Six Research Areas

Alrohaimi Theory — A foundational intellectual system exploring the movement from latency to cognition, meaning, decision, transformation, and impact.

Cognitive Sovereignty — The capacity of individuals and institutions to understand, interpret, and assume responsibility for decisions in the age of algorithmic systems.

Conscious Leadership — Leadership understood as a process connected to cognition, meaning, decision-making, and transformation.

Complex Systems — The study of systems, their interactions, and the relationships among their components and effects on decisions and transformation.

Existential Resilience — Exploring the relationship between meaning, humanity, and the capacity to face transformation and pressure.

Civilizational Transformation — The study of how societies and institutions move from capability and knowledge toward transformation and impact.
